Our story

One Acre.
Endlessly Growing.

JB Farm & Florals is Judy's one-acre home in Live Oak, CA — and if you saw it, you'd understand. What looks like a house is really a garden that happens to have one in it. Every square foot is working: flowers, vegetables, herbs, fruit — grown with intention and deep knowledge of the land.


Judy is a certified near-Master Gardener and an active Blue Zones contributor — someone who has spent years studying and teaching the connection between what we grow, what we eat, and how long and well we live. Farm to fork isn't a trend to her. It's just how life works.
